Rules (as implemented)
  • Your party ( character, HP each) faces enemies ( HP each). Defeat every enemy to win; you lose when your whole party is down. Pick a character in the bar below the board (or click it on the map) to plan its actions.
  • Each round everyone plans up to AP worth of actions (the bigger the map, the more AP). The enemies plan first; their plans show on the board: a numbered ghost token where a unit will end up (earlier stops appear when you hover that unit, for the character you are ordering, or with the 👁 All steps toggle / Shift key), a thin line with a step number for each ranged shot (it brightens when you hover the shooter, the line or the target square), and numbered ⚔️ / 🛡️ badges for melee and guard. Click an enemy card to keep it highlighted.
  • The battle runs in steps: every unit's 1st action resolves together, then every 2nd action, and so on. Within a step: guards, then moves, then attacks.
  • Move: 1 AP per square, in one go as far as your remaining AP allows (like choosing a ranged target; diagonals count as 1 square). The cost is shown on each reachable square. Guard: 1 AP, blocks one hit landing on the same step it is played (it ends with that step). Guard charge: a guard that really blocks a hit gives that character +1 AP next round only (shown as a gold, blinking dot — use it or lose it). The bonus never exceeds +1, but it is repeatable: guard successfully again (even in a bonus round) to earn it again.
  • Melee: 1 AP, hits any opponent in your own square.
  • Ranged: 1 + n AP, where n = squares to the target (diagonals count as 1). It can't target your own square, so the cheapest is 2 AP (neighbouring square), and with AP you can reach at most square(s) away. Hover a target to see the highlighted track and distance. It hits any opponent standing on the target square when it fires. Cost is measured from where you will be at that point of your plan.
  • You can't shoot the unit sharing your square (a ranged target can never be your own square). To hit it, melee it, or step to a neighbouring square and shoot from there. Shooting someone in a different square is always allowed. A shot is locked in when planned: if someone steps onto the shooter's square, the shot still goes to its target square.
  • Everyone can share a square. If you melee on the same step as an enemy who is meleeing you in your shared square, the two strikes clash. A clash takes priority, so you don't get to choose a target for that melee.
  • The enemies fight differently. Melee Chaser: chases its target, melee and guard only (no ranged). Ranged Sniper: keeps its distance and shoots; if you get close it backs away first, then guards or swings. Hybrid Fighter (4x4 and up): does everything, prefers melee, but will back off and shoot or guard when needed. 3x3 has a Chaser and a Sniper, 4x4 one of each type, and 5x5 one Sniper plus 3-4 Chasers/Hybrids (always a mix). Either will go all-in on an attack that could finish you off.
  • An attack hits only one unit. If several enemies will stand on the square you attack, you choose which one to hit (the game asks when you plan it).
  • Knock-back: a unit that takes melee damage is pushed to a random adjacent square (a guarded, fully blocked hit doesn't push). After a clash, only the losing side is thrown clear to adjacent squares (each side tends to land together); the winners keep the square.
  • Crowding: at most 4 units (any mix of your side and the enemies) can share a square. If a square is full, a unit that tries to move in is blocked and stays where it was; when several units race for the last spot, a random one gets it. Knock-back also avoids full squares.
  • Clash: when player and enemy melees fire on the same square, the bigger side is split evenly over the smaller side and each share is its own clash. 2 vs 2 gives two 1-vs-1 clashes; 1 vs 3 gives one hard 1-vs-3 clash; 3 vs 1 (allies striking together) gives one 3-vs-1 clash. The clashing square zooms in and a row of prompts floats above it (3–6 per enemy, summed over the enemies in that clash): left-click on a ⚔️ prompt, right-click on a 🛡️ prompt. Two wrong clicks and you lose that clash. If you win, every ally's strike lands: each enemy in the clash takes a hit per ally and is knocked back. If you lose, each enemy hits one of your characters in it (at random) and whoever is hit is knocked back. Allies make a clash shorter and the payoff bigger, but never free.
  • Each hit deals damage. Enemies never hurt each other.

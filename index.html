Rules (as implemented)
  • You ( HP) face two enemies ( HP each). Defeat both to win; you lose at 0 HP.
  • Each round everyone plans up to AP worth of actions (the bigger the map, the more AP). The enemies plan first; their numbered action badges show on the board.
  • The battle runs in steps: every unit's 1st action resolves together, then every 2nd action, and so on. Within a step: guards, then moves, then attacks.
  • Move: 1 AP, one square in any direction, diagonals included. Guard: 1 AP, blocks one hit landing on the same step it is played (it ends with that step).
  • Melee: 1 AP, hits any opponent in your own square.
  • Ranged: 1 + n AP, where n = squares to the target (diagonals count as 1). It can't target your own square, so the cheapest is 2 AP (neighbouring square), and with AP you can reach at most square(s) away. Hover a target to see the highlighted track and distance. It hits any opponent standing on the target square when it fires. Cost is measured from where you will be at that point of your plan.
  • You can't shoot the unit sharing your square (a ranged target can never be your own square). To hit it, melee it, or step to a neighbouring square and shoot from there. Shooting someone in a different square is always allowed. A shot is locked in when planned: if someone steps onto the shooter's square, the shot still goes to its target square.
  • Everyone can share a square. If you melee on the same step as an enemy who is meleeing you in your shared square, the two strikes clash. A clash takes priority, so you don't get to choose a target for that melee.
  • The enemies fight differently. Enemy A is a Melee Chaser: it chases you and prefers melee. Enemy B is a Ranged Sniper: it keeps its distance and shoots, and guards or backs away if you get close. Either will go all-in on an attack that could finish you off.
  • An attack hits only one unit. If several enemies will stand on the square you attack, you choose which one to hit (the game asks when you plan it).
  • Knock-back: a unit that takes melee damage is pushed to a random adjacent square (a guarded, fully blocked hit doesn't push).
  • Clash: the clashing square zooms in and a row of prompts floats above it (3–6 per enemy, depending on the enemy): left-click on a ⚔️ prompt, right-click on a 🛡️ prompt. Two wrong clicks and you lose the clash: every clashing enemy hits you and you are knocked to an adjacent square. Otherwise you win: you hit every clashing enemy and knock them back. If several enemies clash with you at once, their prompts add up into one longer row, but the limit stays at two misses.
  • Each hit deals damage. Enemies never hurt each other.
